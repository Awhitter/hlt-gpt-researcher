> "Less burnout, more support for new grads. I feel like I was thrown to the wolves, and it was devastating for me. I cried before and after almost every shift and it is something that most people feel. Check-ins, additional education days, more support on the floor, better patient ratios so when you're short and a new grad you don't have 8 acute medicine patients on nights."

- Source: https://doi.org/10.25071/2291-5796.155 (Witness: The Canadian Journal of Critical Nursing Discourse, vol 5(2) — verbatim study participant)
- Signal: peer-reviewed intent-to-leave study of BC nurses; quoted as representative of new-grad experience
- Date: 2023
- Themes: `preceptorship` `orientation` `staffing` `burnout` `first-year`
