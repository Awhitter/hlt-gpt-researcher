# Content-gap packet — nursingmastery.com, August 2026

A research packet, not a strategy canon. Built 2026-08-13 from the generated
content inventory (`content-inventory.md`, 500 pages mapped 2026-07-31) and the
seeded audience quote bank (`../audience/quote-bank.md`). It exists to be
handed to Katailyst2 Create; it does not draft anything itself.

## How to use this packet (the K2 handoff)

1. **Archimedes** (marketing front door) frames the objective from this packet.
2. **Lila** (campaign lead) picks ONE opportunity below and recommends the
   angle, channel mix, and measurement plan.
3. **Cora/Wren** draft ONE artifact through K2 Create into `/content`.
4. A human approves. Then the rail:
   - Nurse-facing on **nursingmastery.com** → ScraperVault Content Studio
     intake (`channel:scrapervault-nurse-content`) — the default for
     everything below.
   - SEO/brand on **hltmastery.com** → MasteryPublishing — only if a piece is
     deliberately re-scoped to the HLT estate.
   - Social → Postiz, approval-gated.

Nothing in this packet publishes anything. Every send waits for a human OK.

**The shape of the gap:** the site is pay-data deep and tool-rich but
editorially thin exactly where the audience hurts. The quote bank now has
receipts for five pains; the articles section addresses roughly one of them.
The tools that answer these pains already exist — they lack the narrative
front doors that bring a nurse in from search or social.

## What the top-1% version has (and NM lacks)

Best-in-class career destinations pair data with journey narrative: the reader
arrives on an emotionally-true story or explainer about their exact situation
and leaves inside a tool with their own numbers. NM has the second half built.
Missing is the first half — pain-anchored editorial that ranks for the
questions nurses actually type ("applied to residency haven't heard back",
"is $X normal new grad offer", "off orientation feel unsafe") and routes each
into the matching NM tool with live posting data.

## Recommendation (one campaign first)

Start with **#1 (residency black hole)**. Reasons: the receipt is the freshest
and most emotionally specific; NM already has both tools it routes to; no
competitor content speaks in the applicant's own words about the
"Manager Review, then nothing" experience; and it naturally seeds the #7
social series. #3 is the fast follow — it leverages the pay moat (293 pages,
live posting data) that nobody else has.

## Evidence gaps to close before scaling

  land fresh (2025-26) confirmations before a campaign leans on the exact
  phrasing.
- Region-level pay quotes are missing — needed if #3 becomes state-localized.
- No competitor teardown yet (Nurse.org, Incredible Health, Trusted Health
  content arms). Run one audience+recruiting-scoped Mastery Research pass
  before committing the channel mix.
